The program

Explorer Manager runs entirely on your machine and never connects to the internet: no update check, no telemetry, no analytics, no account. It sends no file names, file contents or usage data anywhere, so none of it ever reaches Sebi. What it keeps stays on your computer:

  • %APPDATA%\ExplorerManager\settings.json — your rules, watched folders and settings.
  • %APPDATA%\ExplorerManager\explorer-manager.log — what was sorted, including file names and folder paths. At most about 0.5 MB, plus two older copies.
  • With Autostart on: a value named ExplorerManager under HKEY_CURRENT_USER\Software\Microsoft\Windows\CurrentVersion\Run. Turning Autostart off removes it.

To remove everything, turn Autostart off, then delete the program folder and %APPDATA%\ExplorerManager.
